# Block apps

After a group is created, you can lock everything in that group from Home or from the group details screen.

![Group details screen](assets/group-detail.png)

1. Open the group you want to block.
2. Tap **MANAGE** in the navigation bar.
3. Tap **Lock All**.
4. App Lock immediately applies the shield to the selected apps, categories, and websites.

![Lock menu](assets/lock-menu.png)

You can also lock a single item inside the group:

1. Open the group.
2. Tap **MANAGE** next to the app, category, or website.
3. Tap the lock action for that item.

![Item manage menu](assets/manage-menu.png)

To unlock later, open **MANAGE** again and choose the unlock action.
