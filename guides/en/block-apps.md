## Lock a group
1. On Home, tap **MANAGE** next to the group.

![Group row on Home](assets/crops/en/group-row.png)

2. Tap **Lock Group**.

To unlock the group, return to the same menu and tap **Unlock Group**.

## Lock one item
1. Open the group.
2. Tap **MANAGE** next to the app, then **Lock App**.

![Single item manage menu](assets/crops/en/item-lock-action.png)

Categories and websites have their own lock actions.
