## Create a group
1. Tap **Create Group** on Home.

![Create Group button](assets/crops/en/create-group-button.png)

2. Allow Screen Time access if asked.
3. Select apps, categories, or websites.

![Apps and categories picker](assets/crops/en/apps-picker-selection.png)

4. Tap the checkmark to save.

To change your selection later, open the group and tap **Select Apps & Websites**.
