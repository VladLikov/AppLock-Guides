## Manual lock
- In **MANAGE**, choose the lock action to block access now.

A manual lock stays on until you unlock it. Other rules can still block access.

## Schedule
- **Period** blocks access during the hours and days you choose.
- **Limit** blocks access after the day's usage allowance runs out.

Each app has its own limit. Categories and websites share a separate limit.

## Location
1. In **MANAGE**, tap **Lock by Location**.
2. Choose a place and radius, then select **Inside Area** or **Outside Area**.
3. Turn on the rule and tap the checkmark.

Allow **Always** location access for the rule to work in the background.

## Pause
- In **MANAGE**, tap **Pause** and choose 5, 10, or 15 minutes.

Active rules resume when the timer ends. Pausing keeps the day's recorded usage.

## Unlock
- In **MANAGE**, choose the unlock action.
- If you see **Unlock** on the blocking screen, tap it. If App Lock sends a notification, tap it.

A schedule can block again at its next start time. A location rule can block again the next time you cross its boundary.
