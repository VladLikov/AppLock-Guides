## Rename a group
1. Open the group and tap the pencil.

![Rename and icon menu](assets/crops/en/edit-group-menu.png)

2. Tap **Rename**, enter a name, and confirm.

## Change the icon
1. In the same menu, tap **Choose Icon**.
2. Pick an icon.

![Choose Icon menu](assets/crops/en/choose-icon-menu.png)
